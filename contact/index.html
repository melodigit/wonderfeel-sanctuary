What we share

Wonderfeel Sanctuary is a private land, open by invitation. We are slowly welcoming a small circle of friends, makers and curious minds.

  • Harvest gatheringsThree times a year, by invitation. We pick, cook and share a long table together.
  • Community eventsCreative collaborations, forest walks, shared meals and quiet days on the land.
  • Harvest season notesA few letters a year on what is ripe, what is blooming and when the land is at its best.
